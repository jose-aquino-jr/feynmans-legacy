The people behind the project

Team

We are a team from Brazil, brought together by curiosity and a shared passion for space science. We took part in the NASA Space Apps Challenge to turn open data into something people can explore: a way to find places on Earth that resemble the Moon and Mars, where future explorers can train and where we can learn about worlds we have not reached yet.

Our name comes from Richard Feynman, the physicist who believed that to understand something is to be able to explain it simply, and that science begins with the joy of finding things out. His legacy runs from quantum physics to the investigation of the Challenger accident, and it still shapes how we look at the sky and at the ground beneath our feet. Feynman's Legacy is our way of carrying that curiosity forward.

Our logo is a star wrapped by a Möbius strip. The star stands for the sky we study; the ribbon, a surface with a single side and no end, reminds us that Earth and the other worlds are part of one continuous story. If you want to know more about our visual identity, click here.
