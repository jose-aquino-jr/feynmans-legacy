How to read the result

A starting point for exploration

Similarity is only one part of a recommendation. Coverage is renormalized over available criteria, while diversity and logistics help distinguish sites that are both comparable and practical to operate. Strengths are marked at 75 or above and weaknesses below 50. Forecasts describe the next three months; they do not certify mission readiness.
